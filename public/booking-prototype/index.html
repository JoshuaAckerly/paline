Multi-date pricing
Each date keeps its own seasonal rate, surrounding-gig routing, mileage, sound, and production needs. An additional base-rate travel allowance applies to that date only if its combined inbound + outbound drive time exceeds 8 hours. Once a date is confirmed, its travel component becomes a ceiling: later confirmed route improvements can reduce it but cannot increase it. A documented referral that creates the route-improving confirmed show earns the referring venue/booker Route Builder Credit equal to the realized travel savings. Any repeat-booking discount is reviewed after the full series is built so routing efficiency and commitment length can be considered together.

Routing & travel
The calculation uses the previous confirmed PA LINE engagement as the inbound origin and the next confirmed engagement as the outbound destination. If either side has no surrounding gig, that side routes to or from 360 Gould Avenue, Depew, NY 14043. Mileage applies to both legs. One additional base-rate travel allowance applies only when the combined inbound + outbound drive time exceeds 8 hours.
ROUTE PRICE PROTECTION

Help build the route. Share the savings.

When a booking is confirmed, its travel component becomes a ceiling. If PA LINE later confirms another show before or after it and the new surrounding route lowers mileage or removes the over-8-hour base-rate allowance, the earlier booking gets the realized travel savings. Its travel charge can go down, never up, because of a later route change.
ROUTE BUILDER CREDIT: if the later confirmed show came directly through that venue or booker's documented recommendation, those realized savings are specifically identified as Route Builder Credit.
When savings are created, the affected booking contact is notified and can choose to have the savings returned, keep them as future booking credit, voluntarily reinvest some or all with PA LINE, or split the savings between those options.
